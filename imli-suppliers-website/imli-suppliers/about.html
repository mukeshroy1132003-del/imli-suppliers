We have bought, cleaned and sold tamarind since 2009. The work is simple to describe and hard to do well, season after season.

From a market stall to our own pressing unit

Our founder began trading tamarind from a small shop in Khari Baoli, Delhi. Buyers kept coming back for one reason: the block they opened always matched the sample they were shown.

That promise shaped everything that followed. We now buy pods directly from grower clusters, sort and press them in our own unit, and ship to more than 300 regular buyers across 18 states.

We are still a family business. When you call, you speak to someone who knows the stock in the godown that morning.
