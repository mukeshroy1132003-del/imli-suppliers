How bulk orders work
From tree to truck

Six steps every batch goes through

Each step is checked and signed off before the next begins, so the block you open matches the sample you approved.

Source

Pods bought each season from growers in Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Chhattisgarh.

Sort

Hand-graded by size and colour. Mouldy or insect-hit pods are removed.

Shell

Brittle shells and the inner fibre strings are cleaned off.

De-seed

For seedless grades the pulp is opened and every seed taken out.

Press & pack

Pulp is pressed into blocks, weighed, and sealed in food-grade film.

Dispatch

Bags are labelled with batch and date, then loaded the same day.
