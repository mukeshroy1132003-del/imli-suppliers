Our process

Source

Pods bought each season from growers in Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Chhattisgarh.

Sort

Hand-graded by size and colour. Mouldy or insect-hit pods are removed.

Shell

Brittle shells and the inner fibre strings are cleaned off.

De-seed

For seedless grades the pulp is opened and every seed taken out.

Press & pack

Pulp is pressed into blocks, weighed, and sealed in food-grade film.

Dispatch

Bags are labelled with batch and date, then loaded the same day.

Checks on every batch

  • Moisture test with a calibrated meter, recorded on the batch slip
  • Seed count on a 1 kg sample from each pressed lot
  • Visual check for colour, mould and foreign matter
  • Weight check on 1 in every 20 packs
  • Retention sample kept for 6 months

Storage and hygiene

Tamarind keeps well when it is dry and cool. Our godown is raised, ventilated and pest-controlled every month, and pressed blocks are kept in cold storage in the summer months.

Workers in the pressing unit wear caps, gloves and aprons, and the unit is cleaned at the end of every shift.
