Ordering

What is your minimum order?

500 kg for seeded and seedless tamarind, 1,000 kg for whole pods, and 200 kg for paste and powder.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. First-time buyers get 1 kg from the exact batch by courier. The sample cost is adjusted in your first order.

How quickly will I get a rate?

Within 2 working hours of your enquiry, in writing. The rate is held for 7 days.

Quality

How much seed is left in seedless tamarind?

Under 1% in our Standard grade and under 0.5% in Premium. Each batch slip records the measured figure.

What is the shelf life?

12 months for blocks and paste, 18 months for powder, when stored dry and below 30 °C.

Do you add colour or preservatives?

No. Our blocks are pure tamarind pulp. Paste contains only tamarind and water unless you ask for a custom recipe.

Delivery & payment

Do you deliver outside Delhi?

Yes, anywhere in India by road. Freight is charged at actual cost, or you can collect from our godown.

What are the payment terms?

30% advance to book the batch and the balance before dispatch. Regular buyers can apply for credit after 3 orders.

Will I get a GST invoice?

Every order ships with a GST invoice, an e-way bill and a batch slip.

Tell us the quantity. We’ll send today’s rate.
